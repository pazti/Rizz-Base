Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Rizzbase Studio stores your Projects on your device. It does not send your Project contents or passphrase to Rizzbase.

This policy describes Rizzbase Studio, the Rizzbase SDKs and command-line tools, and the Rizzbase website at rizz-base.vercel.app.

Information handled by the apps

Rizzbase does not require an account and does not operate a hosted database. Studio opens and changes the local files you select. Project data is encrypted in the .rizz file on your device. Your passphrase is held in app memory while a Project is open and is discarded when it is closed or Studio exits.

If you export a JSON backup, that backup contains decrypted Project data. You choose where to save and how to protect it. The SDKs and command-line tools likewise read and write files on the machine where you run them. The Go engine is included in the Studio application; Studio does not need to fetch a separate engine binary to handle your Projects.

Studio, the SDKs, and the command-line tools do not intentionally collect analytics or transmit Project contents, passphrases, or backups to Rizzbase. Keep in mind that other software on your device, including operating-system backup or security tools, may separately access files according to its own settings.

Website and hosting

The website's interactive preview uses sample data and does not connect to your local Projects. The site stores your light/dark theme preference in your browser's local storage. The site does not use Rizzbase-operated advertising or analytics trackers.

The website is hosted by Vercel. When your browser requests a page or asset, Vercel may process technical request information such as your IP address, browser details, and request time for delivery, security, and service operation, under Vercel's privacy policy. Website pages may load fonts from Google Fonts; your browser may send technical connection information to Google when it requests those fonts. See Google's privacy policy.

Sharing and retention

Rizzbase does not sell personal information. We do not receive Project contents through the apps. We do not maintain user accounts or an application database containing your personal information. Hosting-provider request logs are handled and retained by the provider under its own policies.

Your choices

You control local Project files, backups, and the browser's saved theme preference. You can remove the theme preference using your browser's site-data settings. Protect exported JSON backups because they are not encrypted by Rizzbase.

Children

Rizzbase is developer software and is not designed to collect information from children. The apps do not ask users to provide personal information to Rizzbase.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the products or their data practices change. The latest version will be available at this URL with its updated date. For a privacy question, contact the repository owner through the Rizzbase GitHub repository. Please do not include passphrases, Project files, or other sensitive information in a public issue.
