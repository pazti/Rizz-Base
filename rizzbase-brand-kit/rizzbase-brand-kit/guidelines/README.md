# Rizzbase Brand Kit

Local-first encrypted database. Your data stays on your machine.

## Included
- `logos/`: horizontal and stacked wordmarks in light, dark, and accent variants (SVG + PNG)
- `marks/`: sealed-file mark and compact R avatar (SVG + PNG), plus favicon sizes
- `social/`: GitHub header and 1200×630 OG cards (SVG + PNG)
- `guidelines/`: visual quick guide, color tokens, and these usage notes

## Logo rules
- Use the wordmark as the primary identity. The sealed-file glyph is secondary.
- Keep clear space equal to the cap height of the R on every side.
- Minimum digital width: 96px for the wordmark, 16px for the compact R mark.
- Never stretch, skew, outline, add effects, or recolor individual letters.

## Color
- Core dark: `#0B0F0E`
- Warm off-white: `#F7F6F3`
- Text on dark: `#F4F4F3`
- Text on light: `#1A1D1C`
- Rizz teal: `#38BFA2` (active `#22957E`, muted `#A5DDD0`)
- Muted text: `#6F7774` · subtle border: `#D9DEDB`

Use teal sparingly for calls to action, key links, focus rings, and small accent moments. Avoid corporate blue, purple gradients, neon green, and crypto gold.

## Typography
- Display and wordmark: Manrope, 600–700, slightly tight tracking
- Body and UI: Inter, 400–600
- Code: IBM Plex Mono, snippets only

## Tone
Direct, precise, calm. Explain what the product does without hype. Prefer “Your data stays on your machine” over abstract security claims.

## Do / don't
Do use generous space, thin borders, monochrome lockups, and direct language. Don't use memes, database cylinders, padlock clichés, network graphs, neon cyberpunk styling, or decorative letterforms.
